Elastic pendulum solver

This webpage uses the <a href='/RKF45/' link='blank'>Runge-Kutta-Fehlberg fourth-order method with fifth-order error checking (RKF45)</a> to approximate the solution to the problem of the <a href='https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elasticpendulum' link='_blank'>elastic pendulum</a>

Where:

  • zzz is the extension of the pendulum beyond its rest length (in metres).

  • θ\thetaθ is the angle of the pendulum relative to the positive xxx-axis.

  • ggg is the acceleration due to gravity (in metres per second squared).

  • l0l_0l0​ is the rest length of the pendulum.
