## Product notation
When we wish to denote a multi-term product, we can use the product notation $\displaystyle \prod_{i=1}^n a_i$. This essentially says that we multiply $a_1$, $a_2$, $a_3$ and so on until $a_n$. It is similar to the summation notation $\displaystyle \sum_{i=1}^n a_i$, with the difference being that instead of addition signs being between each term we have multiplication signs instead. 

For instance, for the product $\displaystyle \prod_{i=1}^n 2i-1$ we would multiply $2i-1$ for $i=1$, $i=2$, $i=3$, and so forth up until $i=n$. If we set $n=3$, then:
\begin{align*}
\displaystyle \prod_{i=1}^3 2i-1 &= (2(1)-1) \times (2(2)-1) \times (2(3)-1) \\
&= 1 \times 3 \times 5 \\
&= 15.
\end{align*}
