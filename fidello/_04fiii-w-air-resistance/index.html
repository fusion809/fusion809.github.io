With air resistance

For the sake of simplicity, we will only consider the air resistance experienced by the pendulum bob itself, not by the rod. The air resistance force vector for the pendulum bob would be:

FD=−ρCDA∣v∣v2.\begin{aligned} \textbf{F}_D &= -\dfrac{\rho C_D A|v|\textbf{v}}{2}. \end{aligned}FD​​=−2ρCD​A∣v∣v​.​

Where v\textbf{v}v is the velocity vector of the bob. It is equal to (x˙,y˙)(\dot{x}, \dot{y})(x˙,y˙​). Hence:

FD=−ρCDAl∣θ˙∣2(x˙,y˙)=−ρCDAl∣θ˙∣2(−lθ˙sin⁡θ,lθ˙cos⁡θ)=ρCDAl2∣θ˙∣θ˙2(sin⁡θ,−cos⁡θ).\begin{aligned} \textbf{F}_D &= -\dfrac{\rho C_D Al|\dot{\theta}|}{2}(\dot{x}, \dot{y}) \\ &= -\dfrac{\rho C_D Al|\dot{\theta}|}{2} (-l\dot{\theta}\sin{\theta}, l\dot{\theta}\cos{\theta}) \\ &= \dfrac{\rho C_D Al^2|\dot{\theta}|\dot{\theta}}{2} (\sin{\theta}, -\cos{\theta}). \end{aligned}FD​​=−2ρCD​Al∣θ˙∣​(x˙,y˙​)=−2ρCD​Al∣θ˙∣​(−lθ˙sinθ,lθ˙cosθ)=2ρCD​Al2∣θ˙∣θ˙​(sinθ,−cosθ).​

To find the generalized force term, we need to find ∂r∂θ\dfrac{\partial \textbf{r}}{\partial \theta}∂θ∂r​:

∂r∂θ=(∂x∂θ,∂y∂θ)=(−lsin⁡θ,lcos⁡θ).\begin{aligned} \dfrac{\partial \textbf{r}}{\partial \theta} &= \left(\dfrac{\partial x}{\partial \theta}, \dfrac{\partial y}{\partial \theta}\right) \\ &= (-l\sin{\theta}, l\cos{\theta}). \end{aligned}∂θ∂r​​=(∂θ∂x​,∂θ∂y​)=(−lsinθ,lcosθ).​

Hence:

Qθ=FD⋅∂r∂θ=ρCDAl2∣θ˙∣θ˙2(sin⁡θ,−cos⁡θ)⋅(−lsin⁡θ,lcos⁡θ)=ρCDAl2∣θ˙∣θ˙2(−lsin⁡2θ−lcos⁡2θ)=−ρCDAl3∣θ˙∣θ˙2.\begin{aligned} Q_{\theta} &= \textbf{F}_D \cdot \dfrac{\partial \textbf{r}}{\partial \theta} \\ &= \dfrac{\rho C_D Al^2|\dot{\theta}|\dot{\theta}}{2} (\sin{\theta}, -\cos{\theta}) \cdot (-l\sin{\theta}, l\cos{\theta}) \\ &= \dfrac{\rho C_D Al^2|\dot{\theta}|\dot{\theta}}{2}(-l\sin^2{\theta}-l\cos^2{\theta}) \\ &= -\dfrac{\rho C_D Al^3|\dot{\theta}|\dot{\theta}}{2}. \end{aligned}Qθ​​=FD​⋅∂θ∂r​=2ρCD​Al2∣θ˙∣θ˙​(sinθ,−cosθ)⋅(−lsinθ,lcosθ)=2ρCD​Al2∣θ˙∣θ˙​(−lsin2θ−lcos2θ)=−2ρCD​Al3∣θ˙∣θ˙​.​
