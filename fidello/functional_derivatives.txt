The left-hand side of Equations \eqref{ELE} can also be expressed in terms of the functional derivative of $S$ with respect to $q_i$. Namely:
\begin{align*}
\dfrac{\delta S}{\delta q_i} &= \dfrac{\partial L}{\partial q_i} - \dfrac{d}{dt} \dfrac{\partial L}{\partial \dot{q}_i}.
\end{align*}
As you can see, this is merely the negative of the left-hand side of Equations \eqref{ELE}. Hence Equations \eqref{ELE} can be written as:
\begin{align*}
\dfrac{\delta S}{\delta q_i} &= 0.
\end{align*}
