## Symplectic integration
There is a class of methods for numerically integrating Hamilton's equations specifically; they are called symplectic integration schemes. Unlike the numerical integration schemes we have previously covered, symplectic integration schemes are designed to specifically keep the Hamiltonian unchanged over time. Other numerical integration techniques when applied to Hamilton's equations with small step sizes or low error tolerances will largely leave the Hamiltonian unchanged in the short-term, simply because the differential equation itself keeps the Hamiltonian unchanged. Over the long-term small errors from approximating the solution with these schemes can accumulate to markedly change the Hamiltonian, however.
