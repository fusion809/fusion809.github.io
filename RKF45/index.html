Runge-Kutta-Fehlberg 4/5th-order method

The Runge-Kutta-Fehlberg 4th-order method with 5th-order error checking (RKF45) is a numerical integration technique for ordinary differential equations (ODEs) that utilizes an adaptive step size to achieve a prescribed error tolerance, ϵ\epsilonϵ. It is used by each page in this website that solves an ODE system. In it, we assume our ODE system has the form

dxdt=f(x,t).\begin{aligned} \dfrac{d\mathbf{x}}{dt} &= f(\mathbf{x}, t). \\ \end{aligned}dtdx​​=f(x,t).​

To numerically integrate this system, we discretize our domain of ttt values. We will not know how many points we discretize our domain into until after we have finished the integration, due to our adaptive step size.
